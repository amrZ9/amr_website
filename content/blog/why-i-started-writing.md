Most of what I learn building products, running growth, and talking to real users never makes it past a private notebook or a late-night voice memo.

Social media feeds move too fast. You share a real lesson, it gets a few hours of attention, and by tomorrow it is buried under noise.

> If your ideas only live on rented platforms, you don't actually own your voice.

## What this blog is for

This space is my permanent notebook on the internet. Every week or two, I'll publish sharp, honest takes on:

- **Growth & Marketing** — what actually moves the needle beyond vanity metrics.
- **Founder Lessons** — real stories from building ventures like BetyChef and Egy-V, including what worked and what didn't.
- **Product & AI** — how a founder's mindset paired with modern tools lets one person ship what used to take a full team.

## The rule I'm following

No fluff, no recycled corporate jargon, and no writing just to sound smart. Every post here starts from a real observation, a real experiment, or a real opinion worth defending.
